# Editing in the app

People edit in Sail Team. Directus's Data Studio is only for breaking glass, when something has gone wrong and Staff
have to reach the data directly. Don't assume anyone will use it otherwise.

- Anything a person is expected to change has a screen in the app, for every role that may change it. A feature isn't
  done while one of its fields can only be set in the Data Studio.
- Never send people to the Data Studio: no link, button, or message that says to fix something there. The one exception
  is the Directus item in Staff's account menu (`toolsFor` in `src/model/nav.ts`).
- What a person can do follows their permissions from `/permissions/me` (`src/directus/access.ts`). Hide or disable what
  they can't do; don't show a button that fails.
- Agents may change data over MCP for advanced edits, but a screen is still owed for anything people do routinely.
