# Style guide

How Sail Team looks and reads, and the rules that keep every page the same. The rest of this Storybook shows every
component, in light and dark.

The people who use Sail Team are staff, coaches, volunteers, and households. They do everything in the app. Nobody
opens Directus's Data Studio except to fix something that broke.

- [Words](?path=/docs/style-guide-words--docs): how a label, button, or message is worded, and the words we've settled.
- [Layout](?path=/docs/style-guide-layout--docs): where things go on a page, and how tables read.
- [Color and type](?path=/docs/style-guide-color-and-type--docs): the tokens, the type scale, and dark mode.
- [Notes and descriptions](?path=/docs/style-guide-notes-and-descriptions--docs): the two kinds of free text, and how
  each is shown.
- [Editing in the app](?path=/docs/style-guide-editing-in-the-app--docs): every change a person makes has a screen.
- [Shared components](?path=/docs/style-guide-shared-components--docs): what to use before building something.

The pages are MDX in `src/guide/`. Change a rule there, in the same PR as the code it changes. The
[`ux-review`](https://github.com/cyc-seattle/sail-team/blob/main/.agents/skills/ux-review/SKILL.md) skill checks a
change against them, and the [`design`](https://github.com/cyc-seattle/sail-team/blob/main/.agents/skills/design/SKILL.md)
skill follows them while building.
