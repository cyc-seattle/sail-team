# Words

Every word a person reads in the app follows the
[`ui-copy`](https://github.com/cyc-seattle/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/ui-copy/SKILL.md) skill: plain words a staffer
would say out loud, the name people already know, no invented terms, sentence case, buttons that say what they do. This
page adds what Sail Team has settled on top of it.

## Say less

A label is the shortest name people already use for the thing. If a header wraps, the words are too long, not the
column too narrow.

- "Program fees", not "What a sailor pays".
- "Enrollment", not "Sailors and days" or "Sailors by session".
- "Staff levels", not "Coach and staff pay".
- "Duplicate", not "Start a draft from this".
- "Allocated" and "Unallocated", not "To programs" and "No program pays".

No parentheticals in a label or a line's name. "Card fees", not "Card fees paid (ASSUMPTIONS)". If the bracket repeats
the name, drop it. If it says something new, it belongs in a note.

A label or a line's name never says where its numbers came from, such as the treasurer's spreadsheet or a column in
it. A note may, and a note that links to the spreadsheet a plan came from is welcome.

## No filler

A page has a title and no subtitle. A section has a heading and no sentence under it explaining the section. A dialog
asks its question and stops. Cut sentences such as "Its numbers never change" or "Each year's Financial Plans: the
Operations Plan and Program Plans, and the P&L they make together": they describe what's already on screen.

## One word for one thing

Once a thing has a name, it has that name on every page, in every table header, filter, menu, badge, and message. Two
names make a reader think there are two things. Before you name something, search `src/` for what it's already called.

For example, a plan has one date it's active from. It is "Active" in the list's column, in its filter, in its badge, and
in the menu item that sets it, never "In force since" in one place and "Active" in another.

Name things the way the people who run them do. A staff level is a "staff level", not a "coach level", because an
instructor and a coach are different staff levels. Offerings are numbered the way the Overview numbers them, such as
"Guppies 3", with their term and dates on hover, never "J-Pod 1" with nothing to say which one it is.
